How to read this

This is a content inventory, not search rankings. It answers “what language is actually on the site today.”

1Counts are editorial uses

Repeated identical copy on the same page was counted once. Asset URLs, implementation strings, and template placeholders were excluded.

2Prominence is visibility, not traffic

Metadata title/description counts 4×. H1–H3 headings count 3×. Body and CMS copy count 1×. A higher score means the word sits in more visible copy.

3The directory is a second vocabulary

Listing types, areas, and amenities are structured keywords in the listings CMS. They power filters and card copy, even when they barely appear in page titles.

Nelson is the sitewide anchor

“Nelson” appears across 16 page/collection scopes with the highest prominence score (124). Brand identity is doing most of the keyword work.

Explore and stay lead visitor intent

Those words sit in titles and headings. Lodging specifics — cabins, cottages, campgrounds — show up mainly on the Stay experience.

Activity language is present but siloed

Breweries, events, trails, wineries, restaurants, and outdoor mostly live on their topic pages rather than repeating sitewide.

Nearby destinations

Search-facing phrases for people looking for things to do near Charlottesville, Lynchburg, and Staunton.

Regional search themes

These are in the metadata.

  • Things to do near Charlottesville, VA Nearby city
  • Things to do near Lynchburg, VA Nearby city
  • Things to do near Staunton, VA Nearby city
  • Nelson County, Virginia Place name
  • Blue Ridge outdoor adventures Region
  • Hiking, waterfalls, scenic drives, attractions, breweries, wineries, dining, events, and places to stay Activity cluster

Who this is for

Drive-market visitors who search from the three closest cities, plus people looking for Nelson County as a Blue Ridge weekend rather than a single attraction.

What this does not do

These phrases improve relevance signals. They do not guarantee rankings, impressions, or placement in search results.

Stay is the directory’s real keyword center

195 of 379 public listings are Stay. Cabins & cottages (90) and whole-house rentals (70) dwarf campgrounds (2).

Blue Ridge is common in listings; nearby cities are uneven

“Blue Ridge” appears on 125 listings and “Nelson County” on 80. Charlottesville is named on 36 listings, Lynchburg on 12, Staunton on 7.

Pet friendly, hiking, and wine are working filters

Pet Friendly (197), Hiking (100), and Wine (49) are among the strongest amenity keywords. Dark Skies Participant is on 16 listings; Meeting Planners on 13.

Culture and campgrounds are thin in both places

Culture has 8 listings. Campgrounds & glamping has 2. That matches the light treatment of those words in site titles.

Notes

This does not include Google Search Console data, traffic, click-through rate, rankings, or keyword difficulty. Private listings are omitted from the public feed, so they are not in the directory counts. Nearby-destination phrases in metadata do not guarantee search placement.
