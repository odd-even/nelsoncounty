Nelson County Tourism

Internal Web Design Brief

Project

Check Into Nelson: A Collaborative Overnight Itinerary Campaign

Client

Nelson County Tourism

O+E Scope

Create one campaign landing page that serves as the central destination for paid ads, organic social, partner promotion, and visitor engagement. The approved scope includes an itinerary-style structure, tourism copywriting, partner listings, possible promo code integration, a simple conversion flow, mobile optimization, analytics setup, and a campaign-specific look and feel.

Project Overview

“Check Into Nelson” is a collaborative tourism campaign designed to increase overnight visitation and encourage travelers to visit multiple businesses during one Nelson County trip.

The campaign centers around a curated wine-country weekend that combines:

lodging

wineries and craft beverage

artisan experiences

outdoor recreation

transportation support

The central campaign idea is to make Nelson County feel like an easy, complete Blue Ridge getaway rather than a collection of separate attractions. Visitors will be encouraged to stay overnight, follow the itinerary, and use the shared promotional code CHECKINTONELSON at participating businesses.

Primary Website Goal

The landing page should convert campaign interest into measurable visitor action.

At minimum, the page needs to help users:

understand what “Check Into Nelson” is

see how the participating businesses fit together

imagine and plan a complete overnight trip

understand how to use the promotional code

click through to lodging, partner, or booking information

move through the experience without needing to research the itinerary themselves

The grant application describes the landing page as the central conversion point for all advertising and notes that it must help track campaign engagement and performance.

Audience

Primary

Adults ages 28–55 traveling with friends, a partner, or a small group.

They generally:

take several leisure trips each year

plan trips within a two- to five-hour drive

value relaxation, scenery, local food and drink, outdoor recreation, and hands-on experiences

respond to curated itineraries and visually driven travel ideas

The strongest geographic markets identified are:

Washington, D.C.

Charlotte, NC

Secondary drive markets include Northern Virginia, Richmond, Hampton Roads, and other parts of North Carolina.

Positioning and Tone

The page should position Nelson County as:

an easy Blue Ridge escape

approachable but elevated

relaxed, scenic, and experience-driven

ideal for a full weekend rather than a day trip

convenient because the trip has already been curated

The experience should feel cohesive and bookable, not like a directory of unrelated businesses.

Visually, the campaign should lean into:

vineyards

mountain scenery

welcoming lodging

artisan workshops

local food and beverage

relaxed time with friends or a partner

The grant specifically calls for visual storytelling that reinforces Nelson County as an approachable but elevated destination.

Recommended Content Hierarchy

1. Hero

Campaign name

Clear overnight-getaway value proposition

Strong primary CTA

High-impact Nelson County image or video

2. Campaign Introduction

Briefly explain:

what Check Into Nelson is

who it is for

why the itinerary makes planning easier

why visitors should stay overnight

3. Featured Weekend Itinerary

Present the experience as a logical trip rather than a flat list.

Possible categories:

Check in

Sip

Explore

Create

Dine

Get around

The exact sequence will need client confirmation.

4. Partner Features

Each partner may need:

image

short description

category or role in the itinerary

location

website or booking CTA

promotional offer, if applicable

5. Promotional Code

Explain:

what CHECKINTONELSON unlocks

where it can be used

whether offers vary by partner

whether the visitor must mention or enter the code

whether a copy-code interaction is needed

6. Plan or Book the Trip

Provide a clear next step.

This could include:

booking lodging

exploring all stops

visiting partner websites

opening a map

accessing the digital passport

The correct action still needs to be confirmed.

7. Campaign and Grant Footer

Potential inclusions:

Nelson County Tourism branding

Virginia is for Lovers logo

Virginia.org link

participating partner logos

required grant or campaign language

The application confirms that Virginia is for Lovers branding and VTC resources are expected across visitor-facing campaign materials where required.

Key Questions for Kickoff

Conversion and Visitor Journey

What is the single most important action a visitor should take?

Is the main goal lodging bookings, promo code usage, partner clicks, digital passport participation, or itinerary planning?

Will visitors book directly from the page or leave the page to book?

Is there one primary CTA or several equal CTAs?

Promo Code

What does the CHECKINTONELSON offer include?

Is the offer the same at every partner?

How is the code redeemed?

Does the page need actual promo-code functionality, or only instructions?

Who is responsible for reporting code usage?

Digital Passport

What exactly is the digital passport?

Is it part of the landing page or a separate platform?

Does the page need to link to, embed, or explain it?

Who is supplying and managing the passport platform?

Itinerary

Is there a prescribed order for the partners?

Is the itinerary intended for one day, two days, or a full weekend?

Does every stop need to be included, or can visitors customize the trip?

Are additional partners being added?

Does the client want a map, timeline, day-by-day schedule, or category-based layout?

Partner Content

What information will be provided for each partner?

Will O+E write all partner descriptions?

Who will supply logos, offers, hours, locations, booking links, and approved images?

Will partners approve their own listings?

Branding and Compliance

Are there existing Check Into Nelson campaign brand assets?

Should the page follow the current tourism website closely or have a more distinct campaign identity?

What are the exact Virginia is for Lovers logo requirements?

Are there any grant disclaimers or required links?

Technical

Where will the page live?

What CMS is the Nelson County Tourism website using?

Who will provide access?

Are there platform or template limitations?

Who is responsible for development and launch?

Does the page need to remain editable by the tourism team?

Analytics

What analytics platform is currently installed?

What actions need to count as conversions?

Is grant reporting handled through GA4, partner reports, promo code reports, or a combination?

Are there existing tracking standards or UTM conventions?

Timeline

What is the desired launch date?

When will final partner details be available?

When will photography and video be completed?

Who has final approval on design and copy?

Working Assumption Going Into Kickoff

We are building a visually rich, mobile-first campaign landing page that presents a curated Nelson County weekend, highlights participating partners, promotes the CHECKINTONELSON offer, and directs visitors toward measurable planning or booking actions.

The most important issues to resolve today are:

the primary conversion goal

the exact visitor journey

promo code functionality

digital passport responsibilities

itinerary structure

website platform and build process

partner content requirements

tracking and reporting expectations
