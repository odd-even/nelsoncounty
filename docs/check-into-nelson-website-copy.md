Need a Place to Start?

A Sample Three-Day Stay

Day One

Settle in and let the mountains do their thing.

Arrive at the Farmhouse at Veritas or Devils Backbone Campground and give yourself permission to slow down. Unpack, pour a glass of something local, and let the Blue Ridge views set the pace for the rest of your stay. No agenda tonight, just good scenery and something local in your glass.

Day Two

Taste, create, and explore.

This is your day to explore. Visit Flying Fox Vineyard for a pour with a view, spend some time getting hands-on at Glass Hollow Studio, and let the day unfold without a schedule to keep. Out here, there's no rush back to anywhere.

Day Three

Head outdoors before taking the long way home.

Save your legs for Nelson's coolest hike: the Blue Ridge Tunnel, a nearly mile-long passage carved by hand through solid greenstone in the 1850s, later reopened as a trail in 2020 after the railroad outgrew it. From there, keep the boots on and chase the cascades at Crabtree Falls, the highest waterfall series east of the Mississippi, take in the view from Spy Rock, or cast a line in the Tye River.

Not ready to leave? Add a night. Nelson moves at your speed, not the other way around.
