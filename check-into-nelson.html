The getaway

A Blue Ridge getaway that gives you time to enjoy it

Nelson County is close enough for an easy drive, but it deserves more than a quick visit. Check in during the week, settle into the scenery, and experience the county at your own pace — tasting local wine, visiting makers, exploring the outdoors, and discovering places worth lingering over.

With participating lodging, attractions, and experiences gathered in one place, planning your stay is simple.

Explore · Day 3

Blue Ridge Tunnel

Nelson’s coolest hike: a nearly mile-long passage carved by hand through solid greenstone in the 1850s, later reopened as a trail in 2020 after the railroad outgrew it.

Plan Your Visit About the Tunnel
6

Explore · Day 3

Crabtree Falls

Chase the cascades at the highest waterfall series east of the Mississippi — five major drops totaling 1,200 feet, with overlooks from an easy paved path to a strenuous 3-mile trail.

Plan Your Visit View on nelsoncounty.com

Spy Rock
