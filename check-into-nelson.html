A Blue Ridge getaway that gives you time to enjoy it

Nelson County is close enough for an easy drive, but it deserves more than a quick visit. Check in during the week, settle into the scenery, and experience the county at your own pace. Spend your days tasting local wine, visiting makers, exploring the outdoors, and discovering places worth lingering over.

Sip Flying Fox Vineyard Small-lot producer known for inventive wines and the Sly Fox vermouth line, letting each varietal speak for itself. Create Glass Hollow Studio Discover the art of glassmaking at Glass Hollow Studio and Gallery, nestled in the scenic Rockfish Valley. Explore Blue Ridge Tunnel Walk the restored 1850s Blue Ridge Railroad tunnel on a 2.25-mile crushed-gravel path with dramatic stone portals. Explore Crabtree Falls Five major cascades dropping 1,200 feet, the highest vertical-drop waterfall east of the Mississippi. Experience Devils Backbone Mountain basecamp brewery pouring award-winning lagers, cocktails, and distillery releases with expansive meadows. Explore Spy Rock Soaring granite dome reached by a hike with rocky scrambles and sweeping Blue Ridge summit panoramas. Stay Farmhouse at Veritas Six luxurious bedrooms in the heart of Virginia Wine Country, with farm-fresh breakfast on Veritas Vineyard. Get around Reliable Rides Spend less time thinking about transportation and more time enjoying your stay between participating stops. Explore Tye River 34-mile Blue Ridge stream offering Class I-II+ whitewater, mountain views, and seasonal paddling windows. Stay Devils Backbone Camp RV and tent sites paired with craft beverage venues, live music, and easy access to hiking and Nelson 151. Stay The Indigo House 5% off your stay with CHECKINTONELSON, plus complimentary coffee service during your visit. Stay Retreat at Crabtree Falls 15% off your stay with CHECKINTONELSON near one of Nelson County’s signature waterfall escapes.
