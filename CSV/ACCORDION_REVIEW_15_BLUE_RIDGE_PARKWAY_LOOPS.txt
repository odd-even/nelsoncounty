The <strong>22-mile ride begins at <a href='http://www.vacabins.com/'>http://www.vacabins.com/</a>Royal Oaks Cabins in Love (Milepost 16)</strong> and goes south to Tye River Gap and back. 0.0 – R Route 814 0.2 – L Blue Ridge Parkway (scenic overlooks into Shenandoah and Rockfish Valleys) 16.2 – L on exit to reach Route 250 East 16.3 – R Route 250 East (Rockfish Gap Tourist Information, long downhill, country store) 19.1 – R Route 750 (Bike Centennial's Route 76) 20.9 – R Route 6 in Afton (home of the former legendary Cookie Lady on your right, just across railroad bridge) Head back up Route 6 22.3 – L Route 250 (watch traffic) 23.5 – R on ramp to Blue Ridge Parkway and Shenandoah National Park 23.6 – L on Blue Ridge Parkway 39.6 – R Route 814 39.8 – Return to Royal Oaks <h5>40 Mile Ride for Mountain Bikers</h5> 0.0 – From Milepost 16 Blue Ridge Parkway – cross the Parkway onto Route 814 Right onto Route 56 to North Fork – follow it back to the Parkway Right onto the Parkway back to milepost 16 <h5>40 Mile Ride for Motorcyclists</h5> For the 40-mile ride, begin at the same point, but head north to Milepost 0 at Afton Mountain. ACCORDION PANEL 1
TITLE: 22-Mile Loop
================================================================================

<p>Enjoy views from any of several scenic overlooks along the parkway.</p>

<p><strong>The 22-mile ride begins at <a href='http://www.vacabins.com/'>Royal Oaks Cabins in Love (Milepost 16)</a></strong> and goes south to Tye River Gap and back.</p>

<h4>Route Directions:</h4>
<ul>
<li>0.0 – R Route 814</li>
<li>0.2 – L Blue Ridge Parkway (scenic overlooks into Shenandoah and Rockfish Valleys)</li>
<li>16.2 – L on exit to reach Route 250 East</li>
<li>16.3 – R Route 250 East (Rockfish Gap Tourist Information, long downhill, country store)</li>
<li>19.1 – R Route 750 (Bike Centennial's Route 76)</li>
<li>20.9 – R Route 6 in Afton (home of the former legendary Cookie Lady on your right, just across railroad bridge)</li>
<li>Head back up Route 6</li>

<li>39.6 – R Route 814</li>
<li>39.8 – Return to Royal Oaks</li>
</ul>
