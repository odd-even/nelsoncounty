================================================================================
ORIGINAL DETAILED DESCRIPTION (THE SOURCE - WHAT EXISTS NOW)
================================================================================

By 2011, Blue Mountain beer was in demand across Virginia at a rate that exceeded 
our capacity at the original Blue Mountain location in Afton. The next evolution 
for us became Blue Mountain Barrel House, a larger production-minded facility 
with almost tenfold capacity of our brewery in Afton. But just building a bigger 
brewery seemed a bit boring. So we did things our way: first, we picked a 15-acre 
spot in the south of Nelson County that has the stunning mountain views to match 
our Afton location. As the most mountainous county in America east of the 
Mississippi, the stark line of the Blue Ridge is woven into the fabric of our 
inspiration. Next, we designed a facility that had special capabilities to produce 
the barrel-aged beers we hang our hat on, beers like Dark Hollow. With a unique 
brewhouse and a fluctuating temperature-controlled barrel aging room, Blue Mountain 
Barrel House operates one of the largest barrel-aging facilities on the east coast. 
Visit us seven days a week in the Barrel House tasting room with inside seating, 
ever-rotating draft beers and a cornucopia of bottled offerings, plus spacious 
outdoor seating with a fire pit and lawn games. We also serve signature Blue 
Mountain food from our food truck from April through November. Join us on the 
quiet end of Nelson County between Charlottesville and Lynchburg and relax in the 
very special setting of Blue Mountain Barrel House.
