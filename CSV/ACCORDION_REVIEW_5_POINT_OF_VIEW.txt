ORIGINAL DETAILED DESCRIPTION (THE SOURCE - WHAT EXISTS NOW)
================================================================================

5 bedroom, 5.5 baths resort house in Wintergreen. Whether you are looking for 
a peaceful weekend getaway, or an active family reunion, enjoy resort living at 
its best in this completely renovated home with breathtaking mountain views and 
a one-of-a-kind huge fenced-in yard, perfect for entertaining large groups, kids 
and dogs. 

With two separate entertaining areas, our house can accommodate lots of fun and 
games. The first floor great room provides a huge sectional sofa (with queen 
pullout bed), large flat screen tv, a pool table (with ping pong table topper), 
a foosball table, and a big table perfect for playing games or doing 
puzzles...and opens to an expansive porch and huge hot tub that seats seven. 
The hot tub is the perfect place to view the night ski and beautiful stars. 

Upstairs, you will find a large, open-plan living area that provides amazing 
two-story views of the mountains. The HDTV and extensive, comfortable seating 
provides a great place to watch a movie or sporting event, and the magnificent 
stone fireplace is perfect for roasting marshmallows or warming up after a long 
day of skiing. The house is fully stocked with games, puzzles and movies galore 
for hours of entertainment. And enjoy the outdoors while relaxing on the many 
seating opportunities provided on the two-story deck. 

The expansive high-end chef's kitchen includes tons of storage and unique 
countertops (made from recycled paper!), an induction cooktop, wall oven, 
microwave, refrigerator/freezer, beverage fridge, two sinks, two dishwashers 
(for easy clean up!) and direct access to the gas grill on the connecting porch. 
Your guests can enjoy your meals at the nearby large dining table that seats 10 
(12 with extra seats!) or from the large kitchen island that seats 4. 

The five bedrooms all feature gorgeous beds and gracious bathrooms. The master 
bedroom, located on the second floor, is our presidential suite, featuring a gas 
fireplace, large hdtv, two sitting areas and sliding glass doors which open for 
cool mountain breezes and the most amazing views of the mountains at sunrise. 
The master bathroom offers a beautifully tiled shower and jetted tub, where you 
can relax, look at the mountains from the private bathroom window, or enjoy your 
favorite show on the flat screen tv. 

The four bedrooms downstairs include a guest master bedroom with queen bed and 
HDTV, and a huge, beautifully tiled shower; a guest room with double bed and 
attached bathroom with tub/shower combo; and, two fun bunk rooms, each with their 
own bathrooms. Additional sleeping options are available on the queen pull out 
sleeper sofa. 

TWO member-lot parking passes are provided to access easy ski-out lots adjacent 
to the slopes.
