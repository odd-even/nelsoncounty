IMPROVED HOURS FORMATTING PREVIEW
====================================================================================================

FORMATTING RULES (IMPROVED):
1. Add <strong>Hours:</strong> label at the start
2. Add line breaks (<br><br>) before the label
3. ONLY add <br> after semicolons (which separate distinct time periods)
4. DO NOT break on day names or commas
5. Keep day ranges together (e.g., 'Monday-Saturday' stays on one line)
6. Keep complete phrases together
