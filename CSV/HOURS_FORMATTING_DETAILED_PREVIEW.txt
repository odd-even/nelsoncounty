HOURS FORMATTING - DETAILED PREVIEW FOR KEY EXAMPLES
====================================================================================================

FORMATTING RULES:
1. Add <strong>Hours:</strong> label at the start
2. Add line breaks (<br><br>) before the label
3. ONLY add <br> after semicolons (separates distinct time periods)
4. Keep day ranges together (Monday-Saturday stays on one line)
5. Keep complete phrases together
