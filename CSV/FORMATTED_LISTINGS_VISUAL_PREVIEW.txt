┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Fishing at Lake Monocan Park                                                                     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Lake Monocan is a 20-acre lake and park located at Stoney Creek in Nellysford. Canoes, kayaks, paddle boats, fishing equipment, road bike rentals, horseshoe pits, sand volleyball court, sandy beach, lounge chairs, picnic area and outdoor charcoal grills are available. Some fees apply.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Hours & Access
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lake Monocan is open Memorial Day Weekend through Labor Day. Wintergreen Resort members and all registered resort guests have complimentary access to Lake Monocan Park. You will need to present a proper ID or pass for complimentary access.

**Public Lake Access:** Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday only. Fee required.

On Saturdays, Lake Monocan is only open to Members, Guests of Members and Wintergreen Resort guests. An access fee cannot be paid for entrance on Saturdays.

For more information, visit wintergreenresort.com (http://www.wintergreenresort.com/lake-monocan/) or call **Phone:** 434-325-8180 (tel:4343258180)

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Fishing the Tye River                                                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The Tye River is a 35.4-mile-long tributary of the James River in central Virginia originating on the eastern slope of the Blue Ridge Mountains in Nelson County, and is fed by the Piney and Buffalo rivers. It joins with the James River near Norwood, in Nelson County, VA.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Fishing Information
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Tye River is home to dozens of species of fish and other aquatic life, including largemouth bass, smallmouth bass and bluegill. Browns, rainbows and brooks can be taken on a fly by the studious angler.

The Tye River is also stocked with catchable-sized trout periodically by the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources from October through May. See the Trout Stocking Plan and Schedule (https://dwr.virginia.gov/fishing/fish-stocking/) for details.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Licenses Required
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ▸ Virginia Fishing License required

  ▸ Trout license also required between October 1 and June 15

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Public Access Points
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Public access points to the Tye River include along Route 56 bridges, from the bridge on Cub Creek Rd. off Rt. 56. However, be aware that permission may be needed from private property owners for nearby car parking.

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Mau-Har Trail                                                                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The blue blazed Mau-Har trail provides an excellent circuit hike in conjunction with the Appalachian Trail through the Three Ridges Wilderness (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Ridges_Wilderness) area in Nelson County.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Trail Overview
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Appalachian Trail (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachian_Trail) crosses through the wilderness for approximately ten miles. This section of the Appalachian Trail, combined with the other trail in the wilderness, the three mile long Mau-Har Trail, creates "one of the best loop trails in the state of Virginia," according to one guidebook.

These trails are maintained by the Tidewater Appalachian Trail Club, a trail maintenance club affiliated with the Appalachian Trail Conservancy (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachian_Trail_Conservancy).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Trail Details
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ▸ **Trail Length:** 3.3 miles

  ▸ **Trail Begins:** On the A.T. 1.8 miles from Reeds Gap/route 664

  ▸ **Trail Ends:** On the A.T., 1.5 miles from the junction of the A.T. and Route 56

  ▸ **Terrain:** Steep terrain passing near the 50 foot Campbell's Creek waterfall

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Shelters
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

There are two Appalachian Trail shelters within the wilderness:

  ▸ **Maupin Field Shelter** - to the north

  ▸ **Harper's Creek Shelter** - 2.6 trail miles from the Tye River

**Mau-Har Trail** - Blue Ridge Parkway (milepost 13.7)

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Fishing at Lake Nelson                                                                           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Lake Nelson is a 40-acre impoundment located in Nelson County, Virginia. This reservoir is owned by the Department of Wildlife Resources and is managed primarily for fisheries related activities.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Facilities
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ▸ Boat ramp

  ▸ Parking area

  ▸ Portable toilets (available March-September)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Fishing
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Fishermen are likely to catch largemouth bass, channel catfish, black crappie, bluegill, and redear sunfish.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Rules & Regulations
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ▸ The lake is open 24 hours a day

  ▸ Outboard motor use is prohibited

  ▸ Electric motors are permitted

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Directions
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

From Arrington: Rt. 655 East (1.4 miles); Left on Rt. 812 (.8 miles) to Concrete ramp

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Access Permit Required
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

_The Department of Wildlife Resources requires an Access Permit for visitors to department-owned Wildlife Management Areas (WMAs) and public fishing lakes effective January 1, 2012, who are age 17 and older, unless they possess a valid Virginia hunting, freshwater fishing, or trapping license, or a current Virginia boat registration._

For more information, visit dgif.virginia.gov (https://www.dgif.virginia.gov/waterbody/lake-nelson/)

Nestled in 11,000 acres in the Blue Ridge Mountains, Wintergreen Resort (http://www.wintergreenresort.com/) is in close proximity to some of the best fly fishing Virginia has to offer.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Fly Fishing Services
