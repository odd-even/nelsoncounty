Welcome to The Blue Ridge Farm & Farmhouse – Your Ultimate Retreat on the Brew 
Ridge Trail! Nestled in the heart of Afton, Virginia, our charming 6-acre farm 
is a one-of-a-kind getaway, offering the perfect blend of history, comfort, and 
breathtaking views. Whether you're here for a peaceful escape or a brewery & 
winery adventure, The Blue Ridge Farm has everything you need for an 
unforgettable stay!

Here's why you'll fall in love with our farm & farmhouse:

A Historic Gem with Modern Comforts
The Blue Ridge Farm dates back to the 1840s, with the majority of the farmhouse 
built in the 1880s. While we've modernized for your comfort, the original charm 
still shines through – think wide plank floors, cozy fireplaces, and rustic beams!

Access to 6 Acres of Gorgeous Land
Enjoy full access to the farm, where you can roam, relax, and soak in the beauty 
of the Blue Ridge Mountains. The scenic views will make you want to stay forever!

Multiple Unique Properties in One Stay
The Blue Ridge Farm includes not just the farmhouse, but THREE other adorable 
accommodations:
- The Blue Sky Cottage – A charming retreat for a couple or small group.
- The Old-New Barn – A rustic barn turned cozy hideaway, perfect for a 
  rustic-chic experience.
- The Tiny House – For those who want to experience cozy, minimalist living 
  in style.

A Perfect Location on the Brew Ridge Trail
Located right along Route 151, you're in the heart of Virginia's most famous 
brewery and winery region. Explore over 30+ local wineries, breweries, and 
cideries, all just a short drive away!

Unmatched Hospitality & Incredible Reviews
With over 1,000 stays and an average rating of 4.9 stars, our guests rave about 
the amazing accommodations, views, and warm hospitality.

Whether you're planning a family reunion, a weekend getaway, or a celebration 
with friends, The Blue Ridge Farm has space and charm for all.

What makes The Blue Ridge Farm & Farmhouse truly stand out from the 700+ other 
homes in the Afton area? It's the perfect combination of history, space, and 
hospitality, with a touch of rustic charm and modern amenities that you won't 
find anywhere else!

1. Rich History Meets Modern Comfort
While many homes in the area are new or recently renovated, The Blue Ridge Farm 
has been a beloved part of Afton since the 1840s! The majority of the farmhouse 
was built in the 1880s, so you're stepping into a piece of history. The charm of 
original features like exposed beams, wide plank floors, and cozy fireplaces is 
beautifully preserved, yet we've added modern touches to ensure a comfortable, 
luxurious stay. You get the best of both worlds — rustic elegance with all the 
conveniences of today!

2. Multiple Unique Accommodations on One Property
Unlike most other properties that offer just one home, The Blue Ridge Farm is a 
multi-property retreat! You can choose to stay in one of four unique 
accommodations:
- The Main Farmhouse – A spacious, historic home with room for up to 18 guests.
- The Blue Sky Cottage – A cozy, private retreat perfect for couples or small 
  groups.
- The Old-New Barn – A charming, rustic barn turned guesthouse with a unique 
  touch of character.
- The Tiny House – A minimalist hideaway for those wanting to experience tiny 
  living in the mountains.
This means your group can all stay together on one property while enjoying 
different styles of accommodation, each with its own charm and personality.

3. Exclusive Access to 6 Acres of Farm Land
Afton is known for its beautiful views, but most properties are cramped or 
surrounded by other homes. Not ours! The Blue Ridge Farm sits on 6 sprawling 
acres, giving you the freedom to roam, relax, and immerse yourself in nature. 
Whether you're sipping coffee on the porch, enjoying a sunset over the mountains, 
or playing games with your group on the lawn, the wide-open space here is 
unmatched in the area.

4. Location, Location, Location – On the Brew Ridge Trail!
The farm is perfectly located right along Route 151, in the epicenter of 
Virginia's famed Brew Ridge Trail. This means you're just minutes away from over 
30 wineries, breweries, and cideries — some of the best in the state! If you're 
a fan of craft beer, fine wine, or cider, our property is the ultimate basecamp 
for your exploration of the local scene. You won't find a more convenient and 
charming home to experience this unique Virginia gem.

5. Top-Rated Hospitality and 1,000+ Stays
With over 1,000 stays and an incredible 4.9 average rating, our guests 
consistently rave about the experience. From the moment you book until the time 
you leave, we go above and beyond to make your stay unforgettable. Our guests 
often mention the personalized touches, warm hospitality, and attention to detail 
that set us apart from other properties in the area.
