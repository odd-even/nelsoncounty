│ 1. Fishing at Lake Monocan Park                                                                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Lake Monocan is a 20-acre lake and park located at Stoney Creek in Nellysford. Canoes, kayaks, paddle boats, fishing equipment, road bike rentals, horseshoe pits, sand volleyball court, sandy beach, lounge chairs, picnic area and outdoor charcoal grills are available. Some fees apply.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Hours & Access
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lake Monocan is open Memorial Day Weekend through Labor Day.

Wintergreen Resort members and all registered resort guests have complimentary access to Lake Monocan Park. You will need to present a proper ID or pass for complimentary access.

**Public Lake Access:** Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday only. Fee required.

On Saturdays, Lake Monocan is only open to Members, Guests of Members and Wintergreen Resort guests. An access fee cannot be paid for entrance on Saturdays.

For more information, visit wintergreenresort.com (http://www.wintergreenresort.com/lake-monocan/) or call **Phone:** 434-325-8180 (tel:4343258180)

│ 2. Nelson County Wilderness Area                                                                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The Wilderness designation provides permanent protection for the two areas in their natural state.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Priest
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Priest (nearly 6,000 acres) rises from an elevation of about 1,000 feet at the Tye River to 4,063 feet.

For more information, visit wilderness.net (http://www.wilderness.net/NWPS/wildView?WID=601) or Hiking Upward (https://www.hikingupward.com/GWNF/ThePriest/)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Three Ridges
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Three Ridges (4,608 acres) is one of the wildest and most rugged areas in the Virginia Blue Ridge. One of the accesses to the area is the Appalachian Trail (https://appalachiantrail.org/) that runs through this scenic wilderness.

For more information, visit Hiking Upward (https://www.hikingupward.com/gwnf/threeridges/) or see the Three Ridges Map (https://ik.imagekit.io/OE/nelson-county/Documents/Nelson-County-Hiking-Trails-Three-Ridges-Final.pdf)

│ 3. Saunders Brothers Farm Market                                                                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Saunders Brothers Farm Market is a family owned business that takes pride in offering our customer service with a smile! We grow quality peaches, apples, Asian pears, vegetables, flowering shrubs, annuals and perennials.

You can find these products and much more at our retail Farm Market, located just five miles from our orchard in Piney River. Stop by and stock up on beef and pork, baked goods, salsa, cider, or jam and in season hand-made wreaths. Try our mouth-watering peach ice cream from Homestead Creamery and visit our onsite farm antique museum!

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 4. Humpback Rocks                                                                               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The Humpback Rocks Visitors Center (https://www.nps.gov/blri/planyourvisit/humpback-rocks-mp-5-8.htm) is located at milemarker 5.8, six miles south of the Rockfish Gap Interchange of I-64 and US 250. It is most easily accessed by either one of these two highways.

Head south on the Blue Ridge Parkway for 5.8 miles (9.3 km) to reach the Humpback Rocks Visitors Center, or continue another 0.2 miles (0.32 km) to arrive at the Humpback Gap parking area on the left.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Directions
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

From I-64: Take Afton Exit 99. Take a right onto 250 East. After 1.5miles, take a sharp right onto VA-6 East. Go .5 mile and turn right onto Afton Depot Lane. 56 parking spaces. No bus parking. Trail fully ADA accessible.
